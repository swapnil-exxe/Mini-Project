# Problem Statement

## Context
In professional degree colleges, campus placements and internships represent a critical transition point for students. However, the path to a successful placement is often ambiguous and challenging. Students must prepare for diverse screening rounds, technical interviews, and behavioral assessments across varying industry verticals.

## The Gaps in Information and Guidance
Despite the high stakes, there is a distinct lack of a centralized, organized, and peer-to-peer knowledge-sharing platform within academic institutions. Specifically, the following problems persist:

1.  **Fragmentation of Experience**: Information about placements (such as interview questions, round structures, and difficulty levels) is scattered across private chat groups, individual notes, or word-of-mouth conversations. It is rarely consolidated for the benefit of junior batches.
2.  **Lack of Authentic Insights**: Public platforms like Glassdoor or LinkedIn offer generic, high-level company reviews but fail to capture the specific context of college-specific campus drives (e.g., eligibility criteria, specific pool drives, and unique round rules set by hiring companies).
3.  **Ambiguity in Resume & Resource Standardisation**: Juniors struggle to understand what constitutes a "successful resume" for specific companies. They lack access to verified study plans, templates, or resources that their own seniors used to succeed.
4.  **Inefficient Senior-Junior Mentorship**: While seniors and alumni are often willing to help, there is no structured framework to capture their journeys. Seniors are repeatedly asked the same questions, leading to communication fatigue.

## Problem Statement Definition
> **How might we create a centralized, academic-focused knowledge platform that captures and structures the end-to-end placement journeys, preparation strategies, resumes, and study materials of seniors and alumni, enabling juniors to access structured guidance tailored to their branch and target industries?**

LOOP (Launchpad of Outstanding Placements) addresses this challenge by providing a clean, responsive, editorial-style knowledge hub that organizes placement intelligence, making it searchable, category-specific, and highly accessible to the student community.
