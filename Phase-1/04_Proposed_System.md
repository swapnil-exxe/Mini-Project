# Proposed System

The proposed system, **LOOP – Launchpad of Outstanding Placements**, is an interactive, peer-to-peer knowledge-sharing platform tailored for academic institutions. It provides a structured environment for sharing and discovering placement resources.

## System Overview
LOOP acts as a bridge between three core student cohorts:
1.  **Juniors (Students)**: Browse, search, and learn from placement stories, download sample resumes, and access curated study folders.
2.  **Seniors and Alumni (Contributors)**: Submit detailed chronological placement stories and share their preparation resources and resumes.
3.  **Administrators (TPO/Faculty/Student Leads)**: Review submissions to ensure content quality and verify placement claims before publishing.

## Core Features and Innovations
*   **Prompt-Based Storytelling**: Rather than writing a single wall of text, contributors answer standard, structured prompts mapping out their college lifecycle (1st to 4th Year, Preparation, and Interview details).
*   **Integrated Resume & Material Library**: Placement stories are directly linked to verified resumes and folders containing topic-specific preparation links.
*   **Branch-Specific Categorization**: Content is categorized under engineering branches (CSE / AI-DS, CE, EXTC) to make it easy for students to find relevant information.
*   **Approval Queue**: All stories and resources are staged in a moderation queue before going public, ensuring data validity.
*   **High-Quality Design**: A premium editorial-style dark interface keeps the reading experience clean, professional, and readable.
